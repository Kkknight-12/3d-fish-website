## New direction
No persistent information cards and no separate opaque creator footer. One continuous red-water scene contains oversized background words, opposing GO / WITH IT entrances, short moving annotations, a drawn body-to-tail wave, an omnivore menu, a large 25–35-year lifespan beat and an eggs-to-fry drawing. Credits and links arrive within that same composition.

The separate water canvas paints moving light contours and expanding rings emitted at the koi's current projected position. Rings remain behind as a short wake. Pause freezes its clock; reduced motion stops it. The original fish geometry, swim, eyes, fins and mouth-bound whiskers remain untouched.

The story retains source links in native field-note dialogs. The egg estimate remains expandable, with its study-specific caveat. Educational claims and creator attribution are unchanged.

## Limits
This is a painted Canvas 2.5D experience. The water is an art-directed illustration, not a fluid simulation. Responsive logical viewports do not establish physical-phone performance. Existing fish anatomy/rasterization approximations remain. Cloud capture can reduce frame cadence; no universal performance claim is made.
